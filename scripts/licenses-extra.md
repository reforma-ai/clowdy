# Third-party licenses

This file lists the open source software Clowdy is built with and the licenses it comes under. The entries up to the table of npm packages are written by hand, in `scripts/licenses-extra.md`. The table of npm packages and the license texts after it are generated by `./clowdy licenses` from the production dependencies of the desktop app and the daemon. Do not edit them by hand.

## Electron and Chromium

Clowdy is an Electron app: <https://www.electronjs.org/>. Electron is released under the MIT license, Copyright (c) Electron contributors and Copyright (c) 2013-2020 GitHub Inc. Its license is at <https://github.com/electron/electron/blob/main/LICENSE>, and the MIT text is under License texts below.

Electron contains Chromium and other components under their own licenses. Their notices ship with the app in `LICENSES.chromium.html`, and Electron's license in `LICENSE.electron.txt`: in the `Contents/Resources` folder of the app on macOS, and next to `Clowdy.exe` on Windows.

## Bun

The daemon runs on Bun, which ships inside the app as the release binary from <https://github.com/oven-sh/bun>, not rebuilt or patched. Bun is released under the MIT license. It statically links JavaScriptCore and WebKit (LGPL-2), TinyCC (LGPL-2.1), and other libraries under their own licenses. Bun's license file lists them with their terms: <https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/blob/main/LICENSE.md>.

## ripgrep

Clowdy ships the ripgrep binary (`rg`) next to Bun: <https://github.com/BurntSushi/ripgrep>. ripgrep is dual-licensed under the MIT license and the Unlicense, at your option, Copyright (c) 2015 Andrew Gallant. Its license files ship beside the binary as `ripgrep-COPYING`, `ripgrep-LICENSE-MIT` and `ripgrep-UNLICENSE`.

## Brand marks

The marks of Claude, OpenAI, Cursor and GitHub appear in the app as icons that show which agent or service is meant. They are trademarks of their respective owners and are used for that purpose only. The Claude and OpenAI marks match the paths of Simple Icons, <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons>, and the Cursor mark is the cube of Cursor's brand kit, <https://cursor.com/brand>. The sources are recorded in `packages/icons/src/logo/SOURCES.md`.

## Reforma

Clowdy also contains design-system code, icon packs and an animation that were adapted from Reforma.
